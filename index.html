Understanding Prostate Health Challenges

Many men experience lower urinary tract symptoms and other prostate‑related issues as they age. Healthcare providers may recommend a combination of lifestyle changes, medications or nutritional supplements to help manage these concerns. Always consult a medical professional to determine the best approach for your situation.

Common Prostate Health Concerns

Many men experience these issues as they age. If you are concerned about your prostate health, consult a healthcare professional.

Common prostate-related symptoms:

  • Waking up 2+ times per night to urinate
  • Weak or interrupted urine stream
  • Feeling of incomplete bladder emptying
  • Urgent, sudden need to urinate
  • Declining testosterone and low libido
  • Chronic fatigue despite adequate sleep
  • Discomfort or pressure in the pelvic area
  • Difficulty starting urination
